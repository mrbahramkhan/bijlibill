Protected Bijli Consumer Kaun Hota Hai aur Kaise Bante Hain?

Updated: 15 November 2026

Seedha jawab: Wo ghar jo pichle 6 mahine har mahine 200 units ya us se kam bijli istemal kare, protected consumer kehlata hai aur use sasta rate milta hai. Ek mahine 200 se upar jaane se ye status khatam ho sakta hai.

Protected consumer kya hai?

Jin gharon ka istemal har mahine 200 units ya kam rehta hai (pichle 6 mahine lagataar), unhe protected consumer kaha jata hai. Inhe baqi gharon ke muqable mein sasta rate milta hai.

Is ka faida kya hai?

Sasta rate lagta hai, aur 200 units ki hadd par aur ke bill mein bohat farq aa sakta hai. Isi liye 199 aur 201 units ke bill mein aksar hairan kun farq hota hai.

Protected status kaise barqarar rakhein?

  • Pankhe aur LED istemal karein, aur bina zaroorat AC na chalayein.
  • Purane fridge ki seal check karein aur bar bar darwaza na kholein.
  • Bijli se chalne wali motor aur iron ko ghante hisaab se istemal karein.
  • Mahine ke beech apne units ka andaza lagate rahein taake 200 se na barhein.

Apne units ka andaza

Hamare calculator mein "Appliances se units ka andaza" wala hissa dekhein, aur units ke saath protected ka tick lagayein. Aap ko turant farq nazar aa jayega.

Dhyan rahe, ye aam tor par samjhaya gaya tareeqa hai. Apni category aur rate ka akhri faisla aap ke bill aur DISCO ke qawaid par hai.
