Bijli ka Bill Kam Karne ke 8 Asaan Tareeqay

Updated: 15 November 2026

Seedha jawab: Sab se zyada asar AC, geyser aur pump se hota hai. Inverter AC 26 degree par chalayein, LED lagayein, bhari cheezein din mein chalayein, aur 200 units ke slab ke andar rehne ki koshish karein.
  1. AC 26 degree par: har degree kam karne se bijli ka istemal barhta hai. Inverter AC non-inverter se kam bijli leta hai.
  2. LED bulb: purane bulb ki jagah LED lagayein.
  3. Fridge: darwaze ki seal check karein aur peeche se hawa ke liye jagah chhodein.
  4. Pani ki motor: zaroorat se zyada na chalayein aur tank bharne par band karein.
  5. Iron: kapre ek saath ek baar mein press karein.
  6. Geyser: timer lagayein ya jitni der zaroori ho utni der chalayein.
  7. Standby bijli: TV aur charger ko switch se band karein.
  8. Slab ka dhyan: 200 units ke andar rehna sab se zyada bachat deta hai, protected consumer ban kar.

Kitna kharch hota hai?

Hamare calculator ka "Cheez ka bijli kharcha" wala hissa dekhein. AC ya geyser ka roz aur mahine ka kharcha ek click mein nazar aata hai, to pata chal jata hai ke bill kahan se zyada aa raha hai.

Ye aam mashware hain. Asal bachat aap ke ghar ke istemal par nirbhar hai.
