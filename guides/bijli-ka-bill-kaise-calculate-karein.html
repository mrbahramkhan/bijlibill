Bijli ka Bill Kaise Calculate Karein: Slab, FPA, QTR aur GST Samjhein

Updated: 15 November 2026

Seedha jawab: Pehle units nikalein (maujooda reading minus pichli reading), phir slab rate se multiply karein, aur uske upar fixed charges, FPA, QTR, 18 percent GST aur TV fee jodein.

Step 1: Units nikalein

Maujooda meter reading mein se pichli reading minus karein. Jo farq aaye wohi is mahine ke units hain.

Step 2: Slab rate lagayein

November 2026 tak base unprotected residential rates taqreeban ye hain: 1 se 100 units par Rs 22.44, 101 se 200 par Rs 28.91, 201 se 300 par Rs 33.10, 301 se 400 par Rs 37.99, 401 se 500 par Rs 40.22, 501 se 600 par Rs 41.62, 601 se 700 par Rs 42.76, aur 700 se upar Rs 47.69 per unit. Alag alag sources mein thora farq hota hai, is liye apne bill se zaroor milaein.

Misal ke taur par agar rate Rs 33.10 lage aur units 250 hon to base charge taqreeban Rs 8,275 banta hai. Is mein abhi baqi charges shamil nahi.

Step 3: Baqi charges jodein

  • Fixed charges: sanctioned load ke hisaab se, bill par likhe hote hain.
  • FPA: fuel price adjustment, har mahine NEPRA tay karta hai.
  • QTR: quarterly tariff adjustment, har 3 mahine mein.
  • GST: kul bill par 18 percent.
  • TV fee: har bill mein shamil hoti hai.

Asaan tareeqa

Ye sab haath se karne ki zaroorat nahi. Units, fixed charges aur FPA/QTR hamare bill calculator mein daalein, wo poora breakdown bana deta hai. Ye sirf andaza hai, asal raqam bill par dekhein.
