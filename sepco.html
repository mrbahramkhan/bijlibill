Fixed charges aur FPA/QTR upar wale calculator ke khanon se liye jaate hain.

💰 Solar Payback

Net metering aur net billing mein farq kya hai?

Net metering mein export kiya har unit import ke barabar gina jata tha (1 unit = 1 unit). Net billing mein aap grid se poore tariff par bijli lete hain aur export ki bijli ka kam "buyback" rate milta hai.

Kya purane net metering walon par asar hai?

Jin ka agreement 9 February 2026 se pehle sign tha, un ki purani shartein agreement khatam hone tak chalti hain (sources ke mutabiq). Naye connection ya baad mein barhaye gaye hisse par net billing lagti hai.

Buyback rate kitna hai?

Alag alag sources Rs 8.13 se Rs 13 per unit batate hain, aur naye connection ka muddat 5 saal bataya ja raha hai. Rules abhi bhi badal rahe hain, so apni DISCO ya NEPRA se tasdeeq karke upar wala rate badal lein.

Ab solar se faida kahan hai?

Faida ab export se zyada khud istemal mein hai: jo bijli aap khud use karte hain wo poore tariff (Rs 40+ per unit) ki bachat hai, jabke export sirf buyback rate par bikti hai. Din mein pump, AC aur washing machine chalana behtar hai.

🧾 Bill ki Lines Samjhein + Late Payment

FPA kya hai?

Fuel Price Adjustment: bijli banane ke asal fuel kharch aur tay shuda kharch ka farq, jo NEPRA mahine ke hisaab se tay karta hai.

QTR kya hai?

Quarterly Tariff Adjustment: har 3 mahine mein NEPRA ka lagaya hua adjustment. Ye bill par alag line mein aata hai.

GST, TV fee aur income tax (235)?

GST bijli ke kul bill par lagta hai. TV fee har bill mein shamil hoti hai. Income tax (section 235) sirf bade bill ya non-filer par lagta hai aur bill par alag likha hota hai.

Fixed charges kya hain?

Ye sanctioned load ke hisaab se lagte hain, chahe aap bijli kam use karein. Apne bill ki "fixed charges" line dekh kar upar calculator mein daalein.

Due date ke baad ada karne par bill par surcharge lagta hai. Sahi raqam bill par "after due date" ke saamne likhi hoti hai.

📣 Galat Bill ki Shikayat Kaise Karein

Step 1: Apni DISCO ko likhwayein

Apne bill par chhapa shikayat ka number ya email istemal karein, ya DISCO ke sub-division office jayein. Bill ki copy, reference number aur meter reading ki tasveer saath rakhein, aur shikayat ka number zaroor note karein.

Step 2: Meter check ki darkhwast

Agar meter tez chalne ka shak hai to likh kar meter check karwane ki darkhwast dein. Is ke dauran bill par adaigi ke qawaid bill ya DISCO se pooch lein.

Step 3: Upar ke idaray

DISCO jawab na de to NEPRA ke consumer complaints ke tareeqe ya Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman) ko likhein. Un ke abhi ke sahi rabte NEPRA aur Mohtasib ki official websites par dekhein.

Is page par jaan boojh kar phone number nahi diye gaye, kyunke ye badalte rehte hain. Hamesha official website ya apne bill se dekhein.

❓ Aam Sawaal

Protected consumer kaun hota hai?

Wo ghar jo pichle 6 mahine lagataar 200 units ya kam use karein. Inhe sasta rate milta hai. Ek mahine 200 se upar gaye to protected status khatam ho sakta hai.

201 units ka bill 199 se itna zyada kyun?

Unprotected slabs mein rate poori consumption par lagta hai aur protected rate chala jata hai. Isi liye 200 units ki hadd par hi rehna faida deta hai.

FPA aur QTR kya hain?

FPA fuel price adjustment aur QTR quarterly tariff adjustment hai. NEPRA inhe tay karta hai aur ye bill par alag line mein hote hain.

Bijli ka bill kaise kam karein?

Inverter AC 26°C par chalayein, LED lagayein, purane fridge ki seal check karein, pump ko din mein chalayein aur peak hours (shaam 6 se 10) mein bhari cheezein kam use karein.

Kya ye official tool hai?

Nahi. Ye aam logon ke liye bana azaad calculator hai, kisi DISCO ya NEPRA se munsalik nahi. Asal bill ke liye apni company ki website dekhein.
