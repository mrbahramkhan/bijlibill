❓ Aam Sawaal

Protected consumer kaun hota hai?

Wo ghar jo pichle 6 mahine lagataar 200 units ya kam use karein. Inhe sasta rate milta hai. Ek mahine 200 se upar gaye to protected status khatam ho sakta hai.

201 units ka bill 199 se itna zyada kyun?

Unprotected slabs mein rate poori consumption par lagta hai aur protected rate chala jata hai. Isi liye 200 units ki hadd par hi rehna faida deta hai.

FPA aur QTR kya hain?

FPA fuel price adjustment aur QTR quarterly tariff adjustment hai. NEPRA inhe tay karta hai aur ye bill par alag line mein hote hain.

Bijli ka bill kaise kam karein?

Inverter AC 26°C par chalayein, LED lagayein, purane fridge ki seal check karein, pump ko din mein chalayein aur peak hours (shaam 6 se 10) mein bhari cheezein kam use karein.

Kya ye official tool hai?

Nahi. Ye aam logon ke liye bana azaad calculator hai, kisi DISCO ya NEPRA se munsalik nahi. Asal bill ke liye apni company ki website dekhein.
