💰 Solar Payback

What is the difference between net metering and net billing?

Under net metering, every exported unit was netted 1:1 against imported units. Under net billing you buy grid power at the full tariff and are paid a lower "buyback" rate for exported power.

Are existing net metering users affected?

According to sources, agreements signed before 9 February 2026 keep their old terms until the agreement expires. New connections and later capacity additions fall under net billing.

What is the buyback rate?

Sources quote anywhere from Rs 8.13 to Rs 13 per unit, and a 5-year term for new connections. The rules are still changing, so confirm with your DISCO or NEPRA and edit the rate above.

Where is the benefit of solar now?

The benefit now comes mostly from self-consumption: every unit you use yourself saves the full tariff (Rs 40+ per unit), while exports earn only the buyback rate. Run pumps, AC and washing machines during the day.

🧾 Understand Your Bill + Late Payment

What is FPA?

Fuel Price Adjustment: the difference between the actual fuel cost of generating power and the cost assumed in the tariff, set monthly by NEPRA.

What is QTR?

Quarterly Tariff Adjustment: an adjustment applied by NEPRA every 3 months. It appears as a separate line on your bill.

What are GST, TV fee and income tax (235)?

GST is charged on the total bill. The TV fee is included in every bill. Income tax (section 235) applies only to large bills or non-filers and is shown separately on the bill.

What are fixed charges?

They depend on your sanctioned load and apply even if you use little electricity. Copy the "fixed charges" line from your bill into the calculator above.

A surcharge is added if you pay after the due date. The exact amount is printed on your bill next to "after due date".

📣 How to Complain About a Wrong Bill

Step 1: Lodge a complaint with your DISCO

Use the complaint number or email printed on your bill, or visit your sub-division office. Keep a copy of the bill, your reference number and a photo of the meter reading, and note down your complaint number.

Step 2: Request a meter check

If you suspect the meter runs fast, submit a written request for a meter check. Ask your DISCO what payment rules apply while the check is pending.

Step 3: Escalate

If the DISCO does not respond, write to NEPRA's consumer complaints channel or the Wafaqi Mohtasib (Federal Ombudsman). Find their current contact details on the official NEPRA and Mohtasib websites.

Phone numbers are deliberately not listed here because they change. Always check the official website or your bill.

❓ FAQ

Who is a protected consumer?

A household that used 200 units or less every month for the last 6 months. Protected consumers get a cheaper rate. Going above 200 units in a month can end protected status.

Why is a 201-unit bill so much higher than 199 units?

For unprotected consumers the slab rate applies to all units, and the cheaper protected rate no longer applies. That is why staying at or below 200 units pays off.

What are FPA and QTR?

FPA is fuel price adjustment and QTR is quarterly tariff adjustment. NEPRA sets both and they appear as separate lines on your bill.

How can I reduce my electricity bill?

Run inverter ACs at 26°C, switch to LED lights, check your old fridge's door seal, run pumps during the day and avoid heavy appliances during peak hours (6 to 10 PM).

Is this an official tool?

No. This is an independent calculator and is not affiliated with any DISCO or NEPRA. For your actual bill, check your company's website.

🔥 Gas Bill Estimator (SNGPL / SSGC)

Domestic gas uses slab rates that rise steeply. Published sources report rates from about Rs 121 to Rs 4,200 per MMBTU depending on category and slab, so enter the exact rate printed on your own bill. The fixed charges listed are as reported for 2025-26 and may have changed.

Protected vs non-protected?

According to published guides, consumers whose winter average consumption is at or below 0.9 hm³ are treated as protected and pay lower rates and fixed charges. The category is reviewed periodically, so cutting use in summer does not change it immediately.

Why can my bill jump suddenly?

Rates rise by slab, so crossing a slab limit can add a large amount even for a small increase in use. Check the slab and category printed on your bill.
