Bijli ka Bill Online Kaise Check Karein (MEPCO, LESCO, IESCO, FESCO)

Updated: 15 November 2026

Seedha jawab: Apne purane bill par 14 digit ka reference number dhoondein, phir official PITC portal (bill.pitc.com.pk) ya apni DISCO ki website par wo number daal kar Submit dabayein. Bill foran screen par aa jata hai.

Reference number kahan milta hai?

Har printed bill ke upar ek reference number hota hai, jo aam taur par 14 digit ka hota hai. Kuch connections par 10 digit ka consumer number bhi hota hai. K-Electric (Karachi) apna alag account number istemal karta hai.

Bill check karne ke 4 step

  1. Purane bill se reference number dekh kar likh lein.
  2. Browser mein official portal kholein: bill.pitc.com.pk ya apni DISCO ki official website, jaise MEPCO, LESCO ya FESCO.
  3. Reference number baghair space ke daalein aur Submit dabayein.
  4. Bill dekh kar print karein ya PDF save kar lein.

Ehtiyat

  • Ek ek digit dobara check karein, ek ghalat digit par kisi aur ka bill khul sakta hai.
  • Aisi website par number na daalein jo login, password ya paise maange. Asli bill dekhne ke liye kuch nahi dena parta.
  • Bill na mile to apne sub-division office se pooch lein.

Bill samajh na aaye to?

Apne units daal kar hamare bill calculator se andaza lagayein aur asal bill se milaein. Agar bill bohat zyada lage to bill kam karne ke tareeqay bhi parh lein.
