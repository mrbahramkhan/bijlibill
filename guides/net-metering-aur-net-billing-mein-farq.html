Net Metering aur Net Billing Mein Kya Farq Hai? (Solar 2026)

Updated: 15 November 2026

Seedha jawab: Net metering mein export ki hui har unit import ke barabar gini jati thi (1 ke badle 1). Net billing mein aap grid se poore rate par bijli lete hain aur export par kam buyback rate milta hai.

Net metering (purana tareeqa)

Aap jitni solar bijli grid ko dete thay, utni hi grid se lene par bill se minus ho jati thi. Yaani 1 unit export ke badle 1 unit import.

Net billing (naya tareeqa)

Ab aap grid se jo bijli lete hain wo poore tariff par bill hoti hai, aur export ki hui bijli ka kam "buyback" rate milta hai. Alag alag sources buyback rate Rs 8.13 se Rs 13 per unit tak batate hain, aur rules abhi badal rahe hain.

Purane agreement walon ka kya hoga?

Sources ke mutabiq jin ka agreement 9 February 2026 se pehle sign tha, un ki purani shartein agreement khatam hone tak chalti hain. Apni DISCO se isski tasdeeq zaroor kar lein.

Ab solar ka faida kahan hai?

Faida ab export se zyada khud istemal mein hai. Jo bijli aap khud din mein istemal karte hain wo poore tariff ki bachat hai, jabke export sirf buyback rate par bikti hai. Is liye pump, washing machine aur AC din mein chalana behtar hai.
